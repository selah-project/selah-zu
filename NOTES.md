# Amanothi okuhumusha — Translation Notes

*Leli fayela libonisa amavesi lapho isandla somuntu noma isinqumo sithinte khona ukuhumusha. Amanothi asele angesiNgisi — akhuluma ngohlelo lolimi lwesiHebheru nezikhombo.*

This file records every verse where a human hand or a ruling touched the isiZulu
rendering — so any verse can be audited: was it machine-pressed under the rails,
hand-rendered, or ruled, and why. Rails: the Selah isiZulu discipline
(uYahwe / uElohim at the Name seat; ⟨את⟩ total; ⟨…⟩ marks supplied words only;
the class-1a u- prefix is grammar, not a change of name).

## The burn and the gleaning (2026-08-31)

The relay rendered 23,161 of 23,213 verses; 52 refused it and were pressed one
verse per call. The census then flagged content faults and every flagged verse was
deleted and re-rendered through the rails, round by round: **1,285 → 265 → 92 → 34 →
18 → 11**. The eleven that survived six renders were repaired by hand (below); after the
Tekoa and aleph-tav re-renders a further 8 survivors were hand-repaired (Hos 1:9, Ps 103:8,
Exod 9:3, Exod 4:18 — tokens written by hand against the 23 Hebrew surfaces — 1 Kgs 9:21,
Dan 1:2, 2 Chr 33:12, Neh 7:5). Final census: uYahwe 5,833 · uElohim 2,157 · every leak class
zero · ⟨את⟩ in 7,423 verses.

## Hand-rendered / hand-repaired verses (2026-08-31)

The stubborn faults were patterned, not random:

- **The ⟨is⟩ class** — an English copula filled into a zero-copula language.
  *Judges 18:6*, *Psalm 3:9* — the bracket dropped (isiZulu needs none);
  *Psalm 16:5* — uYahwe **uyisabelo** sami, the yi- copula the gloss wants;
  *Deuteronomy 4:24* — uYahwe uElohim wakho **ungumlilo odlayo** (אכלה *consuming*
  → odlayo; the render had "odayisa"); and קנא *jealous* → **onomona** — the machine
  had written "onomqondo-omude", *long-minded*, the opposite.
- **⟨אתם⟩ left in the text** — the suffix-pronoun unrendered. *Genesis 40:11* →
  **wona** (the grapes); *Exodus 34:33* → dropped (nabo was already there);
  *Ezekiel 30:5* → **kanye nabo**, and בחרב יפלו *by the sword they shall fall* →
  **ngenkemba bazowa** (the render had a candle); *Ezekiel 38:5* → kanye nabo, and
  מגן *shield* → **isihlangu** (the render had a clay pot).
- **Ascii brackets** — *2 Kings 10:8* (`<…>` stripped; לו → kuye); *Habakkuk 1:16*
  (explanatory `<…>` replaced by the plain literal: uyahlabela inetha lakhe… isabelo
  sakhe sinamafutha, nokudla kwakhe kunonile).
- **Garble** — *Psalm 130:4*: "kuneselichia" → **kukhona intethelelo** (הסליחה,
  forgiveness); תורא niphal → **wesabeke**, *that you may be feared*.
- *Amos 5:17* — מספד *wailing* had been rendered "isihogo sokulila", a hell of
  wailing; → **ukulila**. (isihogo is a rails rejection: שאול is iSheol, never isihogo.)

## Per-token review — the uNkulunkulu floor (2026-08-31)

The census counted 233 verses carrying the common god-word. Read per token against
the Hebrew seat, three classes:

- **True-God seat, singular** (an אלהים-family word glossed uNkulunkulu / kaNkulunkulu
  / kuNkulunkulu, no idol-word in the verse): the common noun leaves the Name seat
  → **uElohim**, prefix kept — **41 verses**, Genesis 1:2–5 among them.
- **Plural at the idol seat** (onkulunkulu, bonkulunkulu, izinkulunkulu: *other
  gods*, *the gods of the nations*, Laban's gods, Micah's, Psalm 82:1, Psalm 95:3):
  **lawful common elohim, kept — 108 verses**. uNkulunkulu stands in the witness
  column, as the rails say; it never stands at the Name seat.
- **Garble** — nkulunkulu written for מלך *king*, שר *chief*, אלף *thousand* (the
  machine confusing inkulungwane with unkulunkulu), אדניך *your master*, תפלה
  *prayer*, אביר *mighty one*, צבאות *hosts*; self-corrections left in the text
  ("—hhayi"); "uNkulunkulu uElohim" doubled: **87 verses re-rendered** through the
  rails rather than pair-fixed. After the round the floor stands at **114**: 113 lawful plurals
  and Daniel 1:2's *the house of his god* (Nebuchadnezzar's — the idol seat, lawful).

## The aleph-tav audit (2026-08-31)

Against the graph's H853/H854 token indices: 156 verses examined; **112 misaligned**
(token count differed from the Hebrew) were re-rendered; 6 missing ⟨את⟩ markers
added, 19 stray ones stripped, 8 sentences patched. Second pass: 2 misaligned (Ps 50:1,
2 Chr 34:17) and 20 glyph-only stray glosses — a token glossed as bare ⟨את⟩ where the Hebrew
token is not את (Ruth 3:9 and 3:16 among them, the bride's verses) — re-rendered; the ten that survived were **את the pronoun** (H859, *you*, feminine —
Prov 7:4, Judg 9:10, 9:12, 13:3, Zech 9:11, Ezek 16:45 ×2, 16:48, 1 Kgs 2:15; and Dan 3:12's
Aramaic יתהון *them*) glossed as the marker; each un-marked by hand → *wena* / *bona*. The
same letters that mark the object are the word for *you*; the audit's truth (H853/H854)
knows the difference and so, now, does the chair. Final alignment: 0 misaligned.
